# Generated scale instances kept for tests

`staircase-3000-seed7.mps.gz` is the output of

    python bench/runners/generate_large_lp.py --structure staircase --rows 3000 --cols 3000 \
        --nnz-per-col 5 --seed 7 --out staircase-3000-seed7.mps

(analytic optimum 10329) at the generator as of commit cb609dc, gzipped. It is the smallest
member of the staircase family on which the interior point reaches the barrier-exhausted stop
(#209, #392): at a relative gap of 2.6e-9 the factorization regularizes 297 of 2,722 pivots
in one step. `tests/unit/test_ipm.cpp` solves it under `algorithm=ipm` and expects the extra
step #392 added to bring the worst complementarity product under the verifier's tolerance,
so the answer is reported optimal and not merely feasible. The 5,000- and 20,000-row members
are generated by the scale runner and are not committed.
